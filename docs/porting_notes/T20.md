# T20 — Clinical States + ReactionProbabilities

Scope: the clinical half of `src/synthea/engine/state.py` (every State
subclass not owned by T19) and `src/synthea/engine/reaction_probabilities.py`.

## ReactionProbabilities (done)

- Ported with its JUnit test (`tests/unit/test_reaction_probabilities.py`,
  6 tests, all passing).
- Java's distribution sampling works on `float` (32-bit) probabilities;
  the port reproduces that with an explicit float32 rounding helper so
  cumulative-probability edge behaviour matches the JVM exactly rather
  than Python's float64.
